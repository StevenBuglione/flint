## Platform and language evidence

- D BetterC language contract: https://dlang.org/spec/betterc.html . BetterC removes druntime dependencies; importing a declaration alone does not prove a Phobos symbol is linkable in BetterC.
- LDC WebAssembly wiki URL https://wiki.dlang.org/Generating_WebAssembly_with_LDC returned an access-denied page in this session. It was not used as positive evidence. Local LDC1.43 compile/link probes and official LDC source are the executable evidence.
- D C ABI reference: https://dlang.org/spec/interfaceToC.html . Platform C type widths and calling conventions must match.
- WASI stable-version/feature guidance: https://wasi.dev/releases and https://wasi.dev/releases/wasi-p3 . WASI0.3 stable status is distinct from a complete D language binding.
- Wasmtime C API installation/linking: https://docs.wasmtime.dev/c-api/ . The exact pinned C header and Cargo manifest govern which features are actually exposed.
- Browser memory/import semantics: https://www.w3.org/TR/wasm-js-api-2/ . Editor draft at https://webassembly.github.io/spec/js-api/index.html was also inspected but is not treated as a final standard.
- Windows console state: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/console/setconsolemode and https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/console/readconsoleinput .
- Windows process-tree containment: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/procthread/job-objects .
- POSIX terminal/process APIs: https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9799919799/basedefs/termios.h.html and https://pubs.opengroup.org/onlinepubs/9799919799/functions/posix_spawn.html .
- Unicode grapheme segmentation: https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr29/ . Cell-width/ambiguous-width defaults in Flint are explicit product policy, not a claim every terminal renders identically.
- SQLite official3.54.1 source ID/SHA3: https://www.sqlite.org/releaselog/3_54_1.html . The downloaded sqlite3.c matched the published SHA3-256 exactly.
- liburing2.15 MIT alternative: https://github.com/axboe/liburing/tree/liburing-2.15 . This corrects assumptions based only on the simultaneous LGPL/GPL files.

## Review conclusions

The design minimizes interface/ABI surface and makes heavy native features opt-in. The evidence supports exact source selection and several locally exercised toolchains. It does not support a blanket “faster than Go”, “all targets tested” or “complete D WASIp3 toolchain” claim. Those claims require the milestone gates and recorded measurements.
