# Review of the low-pause collector design (Gemini 4 Neon High, 2026-11-25)

Independent review of `gengc-design.md`, run read-only in a disposable sandbox copy (paths under `/home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/` mirror the repo, the pinned druntime and the bdwgc/mmtk-core reference clones). Its corrected slice plan (section 5) is the implementation plan.

**Verdict:** Astra’s strategic recommendation—build **Option B (stopped sticky-mark generational GC in D) first** rather than embedding BDWGC—is **directionally sound**, but the concrete design is **not ready to implement as written**. Before coding, six things must change: (1) diagnose `gc:flint`’s existing 1.7–2.05× stopped-mark regression against `gc:conservative` before duplicating druntime’s collector; (2) fix latent correctness holes in `freebits`/`mark` interaction (`gc.d:2644`, `2832`), in-place `realloc`/`extend` (`gc.d:780`, `813`, `840`), and `clrAttr(NO_SCAN)` on segregated/unarmed pages; (3) drop the redundant per-slot `young` bitmap in favor of BDWGC’s single-mark-bit sticky invariant plus per-page flags; (4) enforce nursery-only page allocation so young churn does not dirty old pages; (5) promote `PAGEMAP_SCAN` + `UFFD_FEATURE_WP_ASYNC` from a later add-on to the primary Linux 6.7+ backend (avoiding monotonic dirty-page growth between full GCs) and apply dirty-bit filtering to static roots; and (6) strengthen the verifier with page-checksum dirty-bit checks (`bdwgc/checksums.c`) and post-sweep invariant checks. **Confidence: high** on the correctness findings and architectural tradeoffs (verified directly in druntime, Flint, BDWGC, and MMTk sources); **moderate** on exact microsecond pauses until suspension, 205-fiber stack scanning, and `pagemap`/`PAGEMAP_SCAN` latencies are measured on posidon/venus.

### 1. Correctness: Holes in Option B and Limits of the Verifier

Audit of Option B against druntime ([gc.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d), [threadbase.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/thread/threadbase.d), [lifetime.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/rt/lifetime.d), [blkcache.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/blkcache.d)) and Flint ([collector.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/arena/source/flint/arena/sys/gc/collector.d), [softdirty.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/arena/source/flint/arena/sys/gc/softdirty.d)):

1. **Free-slot `mark` invariant (`SmallObjectPool`) — latent corruption bug.**
   Druntime’s hot `mark()` loop ([gc.d:2644](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L2644)) does **not** test `freebits`; it tests `!pool.mark.testAndSet(biti)` and relies on `Gcx.prepare()` ([gc.d:2832](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L2832)) copying `freebits` into `mark` so stray conservative pointers into free slots are ignored. Astra writes *"Explicit free and reuse reset all slot metadata"* ([astra-gengc-design.md:29](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L29)). On a minor GC that skips `prepare()`, if `freeNoSync()` ([gc.d:1025](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L1025)) or minor `sweep()` ([gc.d:3080](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3080)) leaves `mark == 0` on freed small slots, any conservative hit on a free slot will mark and scan `List.next`/`List.pool` ([gc.d:3126-3129](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3126-L3129)), walking the free list and corrupting reachability. **Required invariant:** free small slots must keep `mark == 1` (`sweep()` sets `mark |= toFree`; `freeNoSync()` sets `mark = 1`), allocation clears `mark = 0`, and `isMarked()` ([gc.d:3492](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3492)) for `blkcache` must check `!pool.freebits.test(biti) && pool.mark.test(biti)`.

2. **`GC.clrAttr(NO_SCAN)` and in-place `realloc` attribute mutation.**
   Astra only notes *"Changes of scan kind, mapping or ownership invalidate cleanliness"* ([astra-gengc-design.md:31](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L31)). Under Astra’s own proposal to segregate atomic pages and read/arm *"only old pointer-bearing spans"* (`clrAttr` at [gc.d:435-461](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L435-L461), `Pool.clrBits` at [gc.d:4081-4099](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L4081-L4099)), an old `NO_SCAN` object `O` can live on an unarmed/untracked page `P`, receive a young pointer `&Y`, and then have `NO_SCAN` cleared. Astra also misses that **`reallocNoSync()` mutates `NO_SCAN` in place** without moving ([gc.d:813-817](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L813-L817)). Any transition from `NO_SCAN` to scannable on an old block must immediately: (a) mark all of the block’s pages as pointer-bearing, (b) insert them into the current epoch’s forced-dirty set, and (c) register/WP-arm them before resume.

3. **In-place array appends / `extend` / large `realloc` into clean `B_FREE` pages.**
   `expandArrayUsed()` ([gc.d:1503-1557](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L1503-L1557)) calls `extendNoSync()` ([gc.d:840-889](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L840-L889)), and `reallocNoSync()` ([gc.d:767-789](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L767-L789)) grows large objects in place, flipping trailing `B_FREE` pages to `B_PAGEPLUS` and updating `bPageOffsets` in C-heap metadata without necessarily touching the base page (`GC.extend` and `reserveArrayCapacity` do not write to page `pn`). If an old large array extends into previously `B_FREE` pages that were never WP-armed or added to the tracked old-page set, subsequent stores of young pointers into those `B_PAGEPLUS` pages are missed. Both `extendNoSync` and large `reallocNoSync` must register/arm and force-dirty newly attached `B_PAGEPLUS` pages on old blocks.

4. **Large objects spanning pages.**
   In `LargeObjectPool`, `mark` and `noscan` live only at the `B_PAGE` index `start = pn - pool.bPageOffsets[pn]` ([gc.d:2679](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L2679)), as `collector.d:3257` already handles for conservative scans. Two gaps remain in Astra’s sketch: (a) promoting a young multi-page object must arm **all** `bPageOffsets[start]` pages (`start .. start + npages`), not just `start`; (b) conservative per-page rescanning works only because D scans pointer-aligned words (no pointer straddles a 4 KiB boundary), so precise mode (`isPrecise`) must stay disabled for sticky minors unless `rtinfo` bitmap offsets are computed modulo `bmplength`.

5. **Old-to-young writes missed by `soft-dirty` (kernel, DMA, `io_uring`, `vmsplice`, `process_vm_writev`, shared C memory).**
   Astra correctly flags async kernel writers (`io_uring`/DMA) and requires pinning referents through final completion (never just cancel CQE, [flint_reactor_uring.c:198](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/engine-reactor/native/ffi/flint_reactor_uring.c#L198)). Note two additional gaps:
   - **Synchronous kernel writes & foreign mappings:** `vmsplice`, `process_vm_writev`, `MAP_SHARED` aliases written from C/another process, and some GUP kernel paths can mutate memory without setting CPU PTE soft-dirty; any old GC block exposed to C/kernel pointer writes must be flagged always-rescan ("shady") *or* restricted to `NO_SCAN` / native buffers (Flint’s `flint_uring_recv` already takes `uint8_t*` bytes, [flint_reactor_uring.c:153](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/engine-reactor/native/ffi/flint_reactor_uring.c#L153)).
   - **Silent kernel `soft-dirty` bugs:** `bdwgc`’s `detect_soft_dirty_supported()` ([os_dep.c:4335-4367](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/os_dep.c#L4335-L4367)) exists because some Linux kernels silently fail to clear/re-set bit 55. Option B must run this runtime probe at init and fall back to full GC if it fails.

6. **Registers, TLS, inactive fiber stacks, and `addRange`.**
   Scanning all roots via `thread_scanAllType` ([threadbase.d:1127-1200](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/thread/threadbase.d#L1127-L1200)) while suspended covers active registers (`callWithStackShell` + `thread_suspendHandler` at [osthread.d:2028-2064](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/thread/osthread.d#L2028-L2064)), `savedRegisters()`, `pAboutToStart`, all TLS (`rt_tlsgc_scan`), and all active/inactive fiber `StackContext`s (`sm_cbeg`). Scanning `roots[]` and `ranges[]` under `rootsLock`/`rangesLock` ([gc.d:3361](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3361)) covers `addRange` outside the heap. This is sound for Option B—with one caveat: if B later applies soft-dirty filtering to static roots (recommended below), **`addRange` ranges must stay unconditionally scanned** unless explicitly proven free of kernel/shared writes.

7. **Finalizers and resurrection.**
   In druntime, `thread_resumeAll()` runs *before* `sweep()` ([gc.d:3420-3442](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3420-L3442)), and `sweep()` invokes `rt_finalizeFromGC()` ([lifetime.d:529](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/rt/lifetime.d#L529)) synchronously under the GC lock (`_inFinalizer = true`) before immediately freeing the slot. Resurrecting a dying object is already UB in D (and `runLocked` rejects allocations in finalizers). Two ordering invariants must be stated explicitly for B: (a) `clear_refs` (at full GC) and WP re-arming (at minor GC) **must happen while stopped, strictly before `thread_resumeAll()` and `sweep()`**, so any pointer stored into an old object by a finalizer during `sweep()` dirties that old page for the next minor; (b) if finalizers are ever deferred off-lock (BDWGC-style), any queued dead young object and its transitive young graph must be promoted before sweeping and reclaimed only on a later cycle.

8. **Thread-cache slots (D1 `ThreadCache` and druntime `blkcache`).**
   Astra rightly defers D1’s `ThreadCache` ([collector.d:221-227](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/arena/source/flint/arena/sys/gc/collector.d#L221-L227)): because TLS is scanned on every minor, unused cached slots would be **promoted to old before the application receives them**, causing every subsequent allocation from the cache to look old, leak until full GC, and dirty old pages. Re-enabling D1 caches later requires either flushing caches back to `freebits` before minor mark or BDWGC’s `clear_all_fl_marks()` pattern ([alloc.c:1362](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/alloc.c#L1362)). Separately, druntime’s C-heap `blkcache` ([blkcache.d:59](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/blkcache.d#L59)) is cleared via `thread_processTLSGCData(&clearBlkCacheData)` ([gc.d:3419](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3419)) and remains sound once `isMarked()` checks `!freebits && mark`.

9. **Promoting every survivor & clearing soft-dirty only at full GCs.**
   Promoting every young survivor in place is **sound**: at resume, zero old-to-young edges exist. Clearing `soft-dirty` only at full collections (while stopped) is also **sound for correctness** (the dirty set is a monotonic superset of pages written since the full GC; even an unwritten `GC.malloc` block on a recycled page cannot hold a pointer to a future young object without a later write that faults and sets bit 55). The problem with clearing only at full GCs is **performance** (see §2), not safety.

10. **Is Astra’s `"verify"` mode a sound oracle?**
    **Partially—it is necessary but not sufficient.** Running an independent full mark into scratch bits on the same stopped snapshot and asserting `fullReachable ∩ proposedYoungDeaths = ∅` ([astra-gengc-design.md:57](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L57)) reliably catches missed dirty pages, bad bitmath, and missed old-to-young edges **provided** the verifier uses an independent root/stack walk. It has **four blind spots**:
    - *Shared root bugs:* if minor and verifier share the same `markAll`/root-registration code (e.g., an omitted fiber stack or over-trimmed `data_roots`), both miss the same root.
    - *Post-mark / sweep corruption:* it runs *before* `sweep()`, so it misses `freebits ⊆ mark` violations, `blkcache` staleness, double finalization, or `binPageChain` corruption.
    - *Silent VDB misses on non-young writes:* if a VDB bug fails to report a written old page that happened to store a non-young word during the test, `fullReachable` still passes; a real run fails later.
    - *Async kernel writes:* an unrooted in-flight `io_uring`/DMA write can arrive after both passes finish.

    **Fix:** Combine the full-mark oracle with (a) BDWGC’s `checksums.c` (`GC_check_dirty`) verifying that no reported-clean page changed its word checksum between collections, and (b) post-sweep invariant checks (`freebits ⊆ mark`, `blkcache` contains no freed blocks, `hasYoung` cleared).

### 2. Performance: Are 35–85 µs Minor Pauses Credible?

**Not under Astra’s Slice 2/3 design (`soft-dirty` cleared only at full GC, mixed pages allowed, whole-root scans).** Astra’s budgets (`33–53 µs` on `/nope`, `57–82 µs` on petclinic, [astra-gengc-design.md:40-44](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L40-L44)) break in four places:

1. **Dirty-page accumulation between full GCs (primary failure mode).**
   Druntime’s `smallAlloc()` ([gc.d:2216-2228](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L2216-L2228)) drains partially filled pages via `recoverNextPage()` **before** taking fresh `B_FREE` pages via `allocPage()`. Because Astra allows mixed pages and clears `soft-dirty` only at full GCs (up to 32 minors), young allocations into free holes on old pages—plus routine mutations to long-lived objects like the 200 `PoolFiber`/`StackContext` structs ([permanent-objects.md:71-72](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/permanent-objects.md#L71-L72)), `Mailbox`, `NativeWatches`, and Spring caches—permanently dirty those 4 KiB pages for the entire 32-minor epoch. Rescanning just 100 dirty old pages scans 400 KB (~3,000–6,000 scattered objects at ~50 ns/object, [m7-gc-mark-census-fork.md:19](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/m7-gc-mark-census-fork.md#L19)), adding **50–150+ µs** and collapsing minor pauses back toward full-GC cost. If Astra’s adaptive trigger then shortens the full-GC interval to 4–8 minors, 12–25% of stops become full GCs, putting 0.5–1.1% of requests back inside full pauses and breaking p99.

2. **`/proc/self/pagemap` read cost scales with total old span, not live objects.**
   Petclinic’s 3.33 MB live set sits in ~20 MB of pools ([web-http-tail-join.md:63](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/web-http-tail-join.md#L63)). `pread("/proc/self/pagemap")` takes `mmap_read_lock` and walks page tables at ~15–40 ns/page plus syscall overhead. Reading 512 pages (2 MiB, tightly segregated old pools) costs ~10–20 µs; reading 2,560–5,120 pages (10–20 MiB, if old objects are scattered across pools) costs **40–200 µs before marking a single object**, alone exceeding Astra’s entire `5–30 µs` overhead allowance.

3. **Suspension, 200 cold fiber stacks, and parallel mark wakeup.**
   - `thread_suspendAll()` ([osthread.d:1470-1506](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/thread/osthread.d#L1470-L1506)) signals only the ~5 D OS threads (`SIGRTMIN` + `sem_wait`), not the 200 fibers—typically **15–40 µs** on 2 pinned CPUs (higher if a thread is preempted).
   - However, `thread_scanAllType()` walks **205 `StackContext` nodes** and **112 KB of scattered fiber stacks** ([web-http-tail-join.md:61](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/web-http-tail-join.md#L61)). Astra priced all 838 KB of petclinic roots at `0.04 ns/B` (`34 µs`), which was measured only on the **contiguous** 529 KB `.data` segment ([m7-gc-mark-census-fork.md:9](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/m7-gc-mark-census-fork.md#L9)). Touching 205 scattered `StackContext` objects and ~190 dormant, cache-cold fiber stack mappings adds ~600–1,500 cold cache-line touches (**10–40 µs** beyond the streaming estimate).
   - `markParallel()` ([gc.d:3607-3659](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L3607-L3659)) buffers roots into `toscanRoots`, wakes a background mark thread via `evStackFilled`, and uses atomic `lock bts` marks. Even on 410 µs full marks, `parallel:0` matched `parallel:1` on 2 CPUs ([web-http-tail-join.md:39-42](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/web-http-tail-join.md#L39-L42)); on a <100 µs minor, parallel wakeup is pure overhead. Minors should run **single-threaded**.

4. **What Boehm’s experience shows about VDB generational GC.**
   BDWGC’s docs and code ([gc.h:1273-1284](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/include/gc/gc.h#L1273-L1284), [gcdescr.md:396-430](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/docs/gcdescr.md#L396-L430), [scale.md:100-104](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/docs/scale.md#L100-L104), [os_dep.c:3287-3318](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/os_dep.c#L3287-L3318)) highlight the exact trade-offs Flint faces:
   - `SOFT_VDB` in BDWGC clears soft-dirty on **every** minor (`os_dep.c:4630`), trading smaller dirty sets for per-minor `clear_refs` TLB shootdowns and first-write faults—the exact fault cost that hurt Flint’s D3 prototype ([gc.md:308](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/docs/spring/phase1/gc.md#L308)).
   - To make minors actually fast, BDWGC applies `SOFT_VDB` to **static roots** too (`os_dep.c:4621-4627`, `mark.c:1711-1736`), avoiding scanning clean `.data`/`.bss` pages (saving ~15–20 µs on petclinic and ~8–10 µs on `/nope`).
   - BDWGC halves its allocation threshold in incremental/generational mode (`alloc.c:401-403`: `result /= 2`), doubling collection frequency and trading throughput/RSS for shorter pauses, and triggers full GCs every `FULL_FREQ_INIT = 19` minors (`alloc.c:147`) or earlier when old-gen growth exceeds `min_bytes_allocd()` (`alloc.c:1390`).

**Realistic minor pause outlook:** With Astra’s unrevised design, expect **~65–110 µs on `/nope`** and **~90–160 µs on petclinic** (degrading across minors). Reaching **~45–75 µs on `/nope`** and **~55–90 µs on petclinic** is plausible *only* with: (a) single-threaded minors, (b) strict nursery-only page allocation, (c) `PAGEMAP_SCAN` + `UFFD_FEATURE_WP_ASYNC` re-arming only dirty old pages (zero global `clear_refs`), and (d) dirty-bit filtering of static roots.

### 4. Option A vs B

**Agree strongly with "B first."** A time-boxed Option A spike is **not** a cheaper first measurement for Flint’s real HTTP workloads, even though Astra missed one important BDWGC capability:

- **What Astra missed about A:** Setting `GC_enable_incremental()` together with `GC_set_time_limit(GC_TIME_UNLIMITED)` ([gc.h:1276-1277](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/include/gc/gc.h#L1276-L1277), [macros.md:619-622](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/docs/macros.md#L619-L622)) disables incremental stepping and runs **pure stopped sticky-mark generational collection**—the exact algorithm of Option B, with `SOFT_VDB` and static-root VDB already built in. (Note: `GC_maybe_gc` at [alloc.c:593](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/alloc.c#L593) still passes `GC_timeout_stop_func`, which sets `GC_parallel_mark_disabled = TRUE` at [alloc.c:979-980](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/reference/bdwgc/alloc.c#L979-L980), though single-threaded minors are desirable anyway.)
- **Why an A spike is still more expensive than a B spike:** To boot `petclinic-rest` or `gs-rest-service` and get a trustworthy HTTP tail measurement under A, you *cannot* skip D’s hard integration work:
  1. `pthread_stop_world.c:835-936` (`GC_push_all_stacks`) assumes each thread’s `sp` lies inside its pthread stack (`crtn->stack_end`). On Flint’s 200-fiber reactor, stopping a pthread running on a fiber stack breaks `hi - lo` immediately—requiring a custom BDWGC build handing STW + stack/TLS scanning to druntime’s `thread_suspendAll`/`thread_scanAllType`.
  2. Petclinic relies heavily on D’s `APPENDABLE` array ABI (`getArrayUsed`, `expandArrayUsed`, `reserveArrayCapacity`, `shrinkArrayUsed` + `blockmeta`), `FINALIZE`/`STRUCTFINAL` (`rt_finalizeFromGC`), and `GC.query` interior pointers.
  3. `policy/upstream-licences.json:42` currently approves only the `SOFT_VDB` source port, not linking `libgc.a`, adding policy + `tools/native_build.py` + Conan recipe work.
- **Why B is cheaper right now:** Flint *already* has a working druntime `v2.114.0` port registered in Boot ([collector.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/arena/source/flint/arena/sys/gc/collector.d)) and Linux `SoftDirty` ([softdirty.d](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/packages/arena/source/flint/arena/sys/gc/softdirty.d)). Using BDWGC’s 1-bit sticky algorithm (no per-slot `young` bitmap), the core minor-GC prototype is **~350–500 lines of D** on top of a 100%-compatible D runtime ABI.

Only revisit Option A (tested first with `GC_TIME_UNLIMITED` stopped generational mode, then incremental) if Option B hits an unfixable pause or fragmentation wall after Slice 3.

### 5. Disagreements with Astra and Corrected Slice Plan

#### Summary of Disagreements

1. **Unexplained 2× regression in `gc:flint` ([gc.md:300-301, 331](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/flint/docs/spring/phase1/gc.md#L300-L301)):** Astra proposes starting a *second* 5,600-line port (`sticky_collector.d`) without first diagnosing why `collector.d`’s stopped mark is 1.7–2.05× slower than `gc:conservative`. If the cause is compilation flags, bounds checks, or inlining differences between `flint:arena` and precompiled `libdruntime-ldc.a`, a fresh port will inherit the exact same 2× penalty. Diagnose first; avoid two 6,000-line collector forks.
2. **Redundant per-slot `young` bitmap ([astra-gengc-design.md:29, 46](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L29)):** Unnecessary 16 KiB/pool overhead and extra hot-loop state. Use BDWGC’s single `mark` bit + 1-bit-per-page `hasYoung`/`hasOld`/`hasScannable` flags.
3. **Broken `freebits`/`mark` invariant ([astra-gengc-design.md:29](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L29) vs [gc.d:2644, 2832](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L2644)):** Clearing `mark` on free/sweep violates druntime’s `mark()` assumption. Keep `mark = 1` on free small slots; clear `mark = 0` on allocation; check `!freebits && mark` in `isMarked()`.
4. **Incomplete mutation hooks ([gc.d:780, 813-817, 840-889](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/druntime/src/core/internal/gc/impl/conservative/gc.d#L780)):** `clrAttr(NO_SCAN)`, in-place `reallocNoSync` attribute changes, `extendNoSync`, and large-object `reallocNoSync` growth must all promote pages to scannable, force-dirty them for the current epoch, and WP-arm them.
5. **Mixed old/young pages + full-only `clear_refs` ([astra-gengc-design.md:29, 31](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L29)):** `recoverNextPage()` will mix nursery churn into old pages and cause monotonic dirty-set bloat across 32 minors. Isolate young allocations onto `B_FREE`/young-only pages during minor epochs, and make `PAGEMAP_SCAN` + `UFFD_FEATURE_WP_ASYNC` (`PM_SCAN_WP_MATCHING`, supported on both posidon 6.8 and venus 7.0) the primary backend.
6. **Whole static-root scans on every minor ([astra-gengc-design.md:34](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L34)):** Leaves ~15–20 µs/minor on the table on petclinic. Apply BDWGC’s dirty-bit filtering (`GC_push_conditional_static`) to static `.data`/`.bss` ranges while keeping `addRange` unconditionally scanned.
7. **Optimistic pause budget & parallel minors ([astra-gengc-design.md:40-46](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L40-L46)):** Undercounts OS-thread suspend/resume, 205 cold fiber stacks, and `pagemap` reads; must explicitly force `parallel:0` on minor GCs.
8. **Verifier blind spots ([astra-gengc-design.md:57](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L57)):** Must add `bdwgc/checksums.c`-style clean-page checks, independent root enumeration in the verifier, and post-sweep invariant checks.
9. **Option A characterization ([astra-gengc-design.md:21](file:///home/olfa/flint-work/agy-sandbox/inputs/astra-gengc-design.md#L21)):** Missed `GC_set_time_limit(GC_TIME_UNLIMITED)` generational-only mode (though "B first" remains the right call).
